# Appendectomy post-operative monitoring (PROTOTYPE)

Not clinical guidance. Replace with licensed sources before any real deployment.

## Days 1–7
- Keep the incision clean and dry unless your surgeon gave different instructions.
- Mild soreness is common; report pain that worsens or is not controlled with prescribed medication.
- Watch for increasing redness, warmth, swelling, or drainage at the site.

## Days 8–14
- Gradually return to light activity as tolerated.
- Contact your care team if you notice spreading redness, foul-smelling drainage, fever, or wound opening.

## Escalation signals
- Severe or worsening pain
- Purulent exudate
- Spreading erythema
- Fever or chills
