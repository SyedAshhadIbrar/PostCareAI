# Surgical wound monitoring (PROTOTYPE)

Not clinical guidance. Replace with licensed sources before any real deployment.

## Visual signs to report
- **Erythema**: spreading redness around the incision
- **Edema**: swelling that increases rather than improves
- **Exudate**: new or increasing drainage (serous, sanguineous, or purulent)
- **Healing**: wound edges separating or not closing as expected

## Infection risk factors
- Purulent exudate with erythema
- Worsening pain out of proportion to activity
- Systemic symptoms (fever, malaise)

## Home care basics
- Follow dressing changes as instructed
- Avoid soaking the wound unless cleared by your surgeon
- Photograph the wound in consistent lighting when remote monitoring is used
